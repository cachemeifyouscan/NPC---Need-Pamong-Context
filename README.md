🎮🧑‍🤝‍🧑🧩 NPC — Need Pamong Context is a gamified learning application developed to support the introduction of Satuan Pengasuhan to Taruna through an interactive and engaging game-based learning approach.

NPC focuses on helping Taruna become familiar with the names, faces, and roles of members of the Satuan Pengasuhan through a series of memory-based and recognition challenges. The concept was developed from a simple but familiar problem: “I’ve seen this person before, but who are they again?”

Rather than replacing direct interaction, NPC is designed as a starting point for familiarity. By introducing Taruna to the people around them through an interactive experience, the game aims to make subsequent face-to-face encounters more recognizable and less unfamiliar—providing a small head start before the real conversations begin.

After all, the game can help you remember who they are. Getting to know them is still up to you.
